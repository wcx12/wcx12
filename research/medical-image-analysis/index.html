Synergistic learning for active learning: A unified training objective for sample-efficient medical image classification

Chenxu Wang, QingTong Meng, Qianxun Lin, Bonan Zhang, Fusen Guo

A label-efficient training objective combining cross-entropy supervision and consistency regularization for medical image classification after active sample selection.

Key result
The authors report improved classification performance and stability on NCT-CRC-HE and ISIC2020 across different annotation budgets.
Scope and limitations
These are benchmark classification experiments, not evidence of clinical effectiveness; the method still requires model training and annotated samples.
